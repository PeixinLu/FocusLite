## ChangeLog

- Added configurable primary and secondary translation languages, with the primary language initialized from macOS preferred languages.
- Improved automatic source-language detection and translation routing: primary-language text is translated to the secondary language, while other languages are translated to the primary language.
- Improved recognition of short English technical terms to reduce incorrect language detection.
- Added target-language switching and retranslation directly in the translation bubble.
- Improved translation bubble positioning in Xcode and other editors with precise selection bounds, multi-display support, and pointer fallback.
- Improved app indexing and settings performance.

## 更新说明

- 新增可配置的翻译主语言和副语言，主语言首次根据 macOS 首选语言初始化。
- 优化原文语言自动识别及翻译方向：主语言内容翻译为副语言，其他语言内容翻译为主语言。
- 提升短英文技术词汇的识别准确性，减少被错误识别为法语等语言的情况。
- 翻译气泡支持切换目标语言并重新翻译。
- 优化 Xcode 及其他编辑器中的翻译气泡定位，支持精确选区、多显示器定位和鼠标位置降级。
- 提升应用索引及设置页面的性能。
